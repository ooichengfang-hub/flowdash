About FlowDash

Public Binance USDT-M futures data only (no account, no API keys). Charts are drawn with FlowDash's own canvas code (no third-party chart library, no CDN), so it also works offline from a file.

Order book heatmap. FlowDash samples the full order book (up to 1000 levels per side) every few seconds and stores binned snapshots in this browser (IndexedDB, 24 h per symbol). The heat shows resting limit orders (liquidity that could trade), not executed trades, and only for the time this app has been open on this device. There is no order book history before that.

Strategy. LONG: sweep below a swing low (wick through, close back above) → absorption or buying pressure → bullish CHoCH (first close above the last lower high while structure was bearish) → bullish BOS (close above the first swing high formed after the CHoCH) → retest of that broken level (within tolerance) → positive candle delta and rising CVD (taker buy > taker sell) → stop loss beyond the protective swing (or sweep extreme) plus 0.25 ATR. OI only adds to the score. SHORT is the mirror. Only closed candles are used, so signals never repaint.

Confidence score = 60 for passing every required step, + up to 15 for OI evidence (new positions in the signal direction 15, covering/liquidation 5), + 5 sweep volume spike, + 5 absorption candle, + 5 strong retest delta, + 10 live order book support (bid/ask imbalance or wall on the right side; live only). It is a transparent checklist score, not a probability.

Times are UTC+8. Not financial advice; signals are rule outputs, not predictions.
